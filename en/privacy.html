3. Information Stored Only on Your Device

The following information is stored only on your device and is not sent to the server. It is deleted along with the app when you delete the app, and when you delete your account in the app, the physique photos and other items on that device are deleted as well.

  • Physique photos: Photos you take or import from your album. They are kept separately for each account. Creating a share image and exporting it to another app or saving it to the Photos app happens only when you choose to do so.
  • Whether you agreed to the safety notice, and the device copy of your language, unit, notification, and sound settings
  • Today's workout progress information shown on the Home Screen widget, the Lock Screen, and Apple Watch
  • The exercise image cache (content that is not personal information)

7. Automatically Generated Routines

Routines and weight and rep suggestions are generated automatically, based on the information you enter and your workout records, according to calculation rules built into the app. You can edit routines yourself and swap or exclude exercises. The app does not make automated decisions that have legal effects on you or similarly significant effects.

10. Retention and Destruction

  • Personal information stored on the server is kept until you delete your account, and when you delete your account it is deleted immediately. Subscription records are deleted as well. Currently, the Operator keeps no information separately after account deletion.
  • Deleted information may remain in database backups, and backups disappear automatically after the retention period (currently 7 days). Backups are used only for disaster recovery.
  • Service usage records (server request records and login records) are deleted automatically, separately from account deletion, after the retention period set by the hosting provider (Supabase). The Operator uses these records only for security and incident response.
  • Inquiry emails are kept for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and answer follow-up inquiries, and are deleted if you request deletion.
  • Payment records are kept by Apple in accordance with Apple's policies and applicable law.
  • Destruction is done by deleting from the database in a way that cannot be recovered.

For how to delete your account, see Account and Data Deletion.

11. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

  • You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, suspension (restriction) of processing of, and a copy (portability) of your personal information, you may withdraw consent, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • You can check and edit your profile, workout settings, language, and units yourself under Profile in the app, and you can delete your account and all records immediately under Profile → Account → Delete Account.
  • Please send any other requests to support@ilak.kr. To verify your identity, we recommend sending from the email address of the account you signed up with.
  • We handle requests without delay and tell you the result within the period set by applicable law. If we refuse or postpone a request, we also tell you the reason and how to object.
  • We do not treat you unfavorably for exercising your rights.
  • You may file a complaint with the personal data supervisory authority of your country of residence (including in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) (Section 17).

12. Security Measures

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Server access control (row-level security) so that you can view and edit only your own data
  • Subscription status can be changed only by the server, and the server's secret keys are managed separately from the app
  • Login and payment use Apple's authentication and payment systems

13. Children's Personal Information

The app is not directed at people under 16 (or under the higher age set by the law of the country where you live), and an age under 16 cannot be entered during onboarding or under Profile. If we learn that information of a user under this age has been collected, we will delete it without delay.

14. Tracking, Advertising, and Cookies

  • The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • The app has no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, or crash reporting.
  • If you turn on "Share with App Developers" in your device settings, Apple may provide crash reports and anonymous usage statistics to the Operator. You can turn this setting off on your device.
  • This documentation website is served through GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), and the app's data is not stored there. The pages of this website do not use cookies. When you visit, GitHub may process access records (such as IP address) in order to serve the website, and GitHub's policies apply to this.

15. Notice for Residents of Certain U.S. States (Including Washington)

The laws of certain U.S. states, including Washington, treat information that indicates a person's health status, including body measurements such as weight and heart rate, as consumer health data. The Operator collects such information only for the purposes in Section 6 and only with the separate consent in Section 6. We do not sell this information, do not use it for targeted advertising, and do not share it with other companies other than the entrusted processing in Section 8.

The Operator also does not sell other personal information and does not share it with other companies for targeted advertising. The types of information processed, the purposes, and the retention periods are as described in Sections 2, 5, 6, and 10. You can request access, deletion, correction, and withdrawal of consent in the way described in Section 11, and we do not treat you unfavorably for exercising your rights.
