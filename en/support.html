Frequently Asked Questions

  • How much can I use for free? You can use all features, including the Apple Watch app, for free until you finish your first 2 or 3 workouts (determined by the weekly workout frequency you set). After that, you need a subscription to start a new workout. You are not charged automatically when free use ends.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? Cancel under Settings app → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone. While you are subscribed, you can also open the same screen from Profile → Manage Subscription in the app. Even if you cancel, you can use Pro until the end of the period you have paid for.
  • I want a refund. Apple processes payments and refunds. Please request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • My subscription doesn't show up on another device. Sign in with the same Apple account, then tap Restore Purchase on the subscription card at the top of the Profile tab in the app. If you restore on a different FitPlanner AI account, Pro moves to that account and is removed from the previous account.
  • It says my payment failed. Please check the payment method on your Apple account. While Apple retries the payment during a grace period (currently 3 days), you can keep using Pro.
  • I want to change the language or units (kg/lb, cm/ft·in). You can change them in the Profile tab in the app.
  • I want to delete my account and data. Please see Account and Data Deletion.
  • How is my personal information handled? Please see the Privacy Policy.
