FitPlanner AI Account and Data Deletion

App name: FitPlanner AI · Operator: ilak · Effective date: October 4, 2026

This page explains how to delete your FitPlanner AI account and the data linked to it. When you delete your account, all consents are withdrawn, including your consent to the collection and use of health-related information.

Method 1. Delete directly in the app (processed immediately)

If the app is installed, this is the fastest method. Your account is deleted immediately without a separate request, and this cannot be undone.

  1. Open the FitPlanner AI app
  2. Select the Profile tab at the bottom of the screen
  3. In the Account section at the very bottom, select Delete Account
  4. Review the items that will be deleted along with it, then tap Continue
  5. Type the confirmation word shown on the screen into the input field (DELETE when you use the app in English) → Delete Permanently

When deletion finishes, the app returns to the login screen. If deletion could not be completed, nothing is deleted, and you can try again.

If you only want to log out, use Log Out in the same Account section. Logging out leaves the records on the server as they are.

Method 2. Request deletion by email (without the app)

If you have already deleted the app or have trouble accessing it, please send a request to the address below.

Address to send to: support@ilak.kr
Email subject: FitPlanner AI account deletion request
Please include in the body: The email address of the Apple account you used to sign in (if you used Apple's private relay address, that address)

To verify your identity, we recommend sending from the email address you used to sign up. If you send from a different address, we may ask for additional verification. We handle requests without delay, and we complete the deletion and reply within the period set by applicable law.

Data That Is Deleted

ItemHandling
Account information (user ID, Sign in with Apple identifier, email, nickname)Permanently deleted immediately
Profile and workout settings (age, gender, etc.), language and unit settingsPermanently deleted immediately
Health-related information (height, weight, weight log, average and maximum heart rate)Permanently deleted immediately
Routines, list of excluded exercises, progress state per exercisePermanently deleted immediately
Workout recordsPermanently deleted immediately
Subscription records (server)Permanently deleted immediately. Payment records are kept by Apple under Apple's policies.
Database backupsDeleted information may remain in backups, and it disappears automatically after the retention period (currently 7 days).
Service usage records (server request records and login records)Separately from account deletion, they are deleted automatically after the retention period set by the hosting provider (Supabase).
Data on this device (physique photos, safety notice consent, temporary share images, workout information shown on Apple Watch, scheduled workout reminders, content of the Home Screen widget)When you delete your account in the app, these are deleted from that device as well. Physique photos are not stored on the server in the first place. They also disappear if you delete the app.

What FitPlanner AI Cannot Delete

  • Workout records in the Health app: Workouts done with Apple Watch are also saved in the Health app. You can delete these records yourself in the Health app.
  • Share images saved in the Photos app: You can delete them yourself in the Photos app.
  • Sign in with Apple connection: Even after you delete your account, FitPlanner AI may remain in the "Sign in with Apple" list in your Apple account settings. You can stop using it yourself from that list.

If You Have a Subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription automatically. Before deleting, please cancel under Settings app → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Apple processes payments and refunds.
